# mast-web v0.7.0 plan

**Status:** **executed, pending the v0.7.0 tag** (2026-10-10). All six PRs merged; see the Issues table. Drafted 2026-10-09 (post-v0.6.0, `0a73930`). Supersedes [`v0.6-plan.md`](./v0.6-plan.md) (executed, v0.6.0 tagged 2026-10-09). Read against `core-agent@99a1f46` (protocol **1.20.0**) and `core-tui@f1bcdf7`.

## Goal

**See what's running.** An operator who hands work to an agent hands it to subagents too, and today mast-web can't show them. There's no view of which subagents are in flight, how long they've been at it, what they last reported, or when a sleeping one will wake. core-tui has had that view for months: a running-tasks bar between the input and the footer. And protocol 1.20.0, which core-agent reached the day v0.6 shipped, adds exactly the fields that bar displays.

This release also corrects a mistake that ran through two releases. v0.5 and v0.6 both said the live subagent roster carries no status, so a running count couldn't be shown truthfully ([#106](https://github.com/go-steer/mast-web/issues/106)). That was wrong. `GET /agents` rows have always carried `status` and `started_at`. The claim came from reading the mock, whose `/agents` returns the *configured* roster's shape (`name`, `description`), instead of the producer. So the first PR is the mock, held to the producer's shape so it can't happen again.

Two small items ride along: inline `/` autocomplete ([#43](https://github.com/go-steer/mast-web/issues/43)) and the silently-ignored `?shell=` typo ([#120](https://github.com/go-steer/mast-web/issues/120)).

**Hosting moves to v0.8**, at the operator's call (2026-10-09): [#86](https://github.com/go-steer/mast-web/issues/86) (oidc), [#66](https://github.com/go-steer/mast-web/issues/66) (Kind against a real core-agent), [#6](https://github.com/go-steer/mast-web/issues/6) (hosted SPA). That's the third time hosting has moved, and v0.8 should say why if it moves a fourth.

## Design overview

### 1. The live roster, and what 1.20.0 adds

`GET /sessions/{sid}/agents` (core-agent's REST reference, `attach-http.md`) answers:

```
{"agents": [{"id", "name", "status", "started_at", "parent_session_id"?,
             "last_report"?, "next_wake_at"?, "wake_detail"?}]}
```

- `status` ∈ `running | completed | failed | stopped | deferred`. core-tui also treats `paused` as active, so mast-web does too and tolerates anything else as "finished, with this word".
- **1.20.0 (#1283):** `last_report` fills in *while* a subagent runs: its latest `report_alert`, or else its latest model message, cut to 1 KiB. Before 1.20.0 it stayed empty until the subagent finished. The display is the first non-blank line. `next_wake_at` (RFC 3339) and `wake_detail` are present only while a subagent sleeps on a `schedule_next_turn` wake, and its `status` stays `running` meanwhile. Treat `next_wake_at` as the latest it will wake, since it can be woken early.
- Absent fields are unknown, as always: a pre-1.20.0 row with no `last_report` is a subagent that hasn't said anything *we can see*, not one with nothing to say.

`client.listAgents()` already exists and nothing in `web/` calls it. `/subagents` reads the *configured* roster (`/subagents`), which is a different question ("what can be spawned").

### 2. The running-subagents bar

A port of core-tui's `tui/tasks_bar.go`, which has already settled the hard choices:

- **Where:** a strip between the transcript and the prompt, in both shells. It's the same slot the hold and halt banners use, and it stacks under them.
- **Rows:** one per active subagent (`running` or `paused`), in start order: a glyph, the name, then **elapsed** since `started_at`, or **"wakes in 4m"** counting *down* when it's scheduled (`next_wake_at`, with "waking" once that's past due: the roster is a poll, and a frozen `0s` would draw stale data as fact), or "paused". Then the first line of `last_report`, or of `wake_detail` while it sleeps, cut at the edge.
- **Linger:** a subagent the bar *watched* running keeps its row for 5 seconds after it finishes, showing how it ended (red for `failed`). A roster read on attach can hold any number of long-finished entries, and flashing them all up would announce history as news, so only a watched transition lingers.
- **Cap:** at most three rows. Past that, the last row reads `+ N more · /subagents`, so the bar never silently claims to be the whole roster.
- **Count:** the status bar gets `N subagents running · M scheduled`, kept apart because "2 running" for two subagents asleep for ten minutes would be wrong. The count is what survives when the bar is hidden: in a small spatial panel, the bar can drop to one row and the count stays.

### 3. Polling, and the cost of it

core-tui's remote mode polls `/agents` at **1 Hz**, through a cache with a background refresh (core-agent `internal/coretuiremote`). mast-web has one panel per session, and a room can hold six, so 1 Hz per panel is a lot of requests for a strip most sessions leave empty. Proposed: the roster rides the existing status chain (v0.5's #93). That's every 3 s while anything is live, which now includes an active subagent; every 10 s idle; and nothing while the tab is hidden. The elapsed and countdown columns tick locally every second between polls, as core-tui's do between snapshots. A 404 (a backend with no `AgentReporter`) stops the poll for that panel, and the bar stays empty without complaint. See open question 1.

### 4. The hold banner's count (#106)

The hold banner gains *"2 subagents still running"* when any are. A hold stops the parent loop and **does not** stop background subagents (interrupting doesn't either; that's `stop_subagents` or `/subagents stop`), and that difference is what decides "safe to walk away" versus "wait". It comes from the same roster read, so it costs nothing extra.

### 5. Inline `/` autocomplete (#43)

#43's text predates v0.4 and points at the deleted `app.js`. Re-scoped: when the prompt starts with `/`, a list anchored under the input shows the same commands the palette (`shell.js`, Ctrl/Cmd+P) lists, filtered live. The source is the same `available()` table, so it can't offer a command the backend would refuse. Up/Down/Enter/Tab/Esc drive it while it's open. When it's closed, Enter-to-send and Shift+Enter are untouched. Dismiss on click-away or when the input stops starting with `/`. The palette stays.

### 6. A mistyped `?shell=` (#120)

The chooser treats an unknown value like no value and silently lands in solo. Proposed: it renders its existing no-JS page (both shells, both links) with one line naming the unrecognised value, instead of redirecting. It needs no state threaded through, and it works under the chooser's `script-src 'self'` CSP.

### 7. What the mock owes

- **`/agents` in the producer's shape**, with a drift guard: there's no upstream conformance capture for this route, so the test pins the key set the REST reference documents, and fails if the mock invents or drops a key. That's the guard whose absence let the v0.5 and v0.6 mistake stand.
- **Subagents on demand**: `POST /_mock/subagent {session, name, status, started_at?, last_report?, next_wake_at?, wake_detail?}` creates or updates a roster row, so a spec can make one start, report, sleep, wake and finish. `DELETE /_mock/subagents` resets.
- **1.20.0** advertised by the default fixture, as the version guard requires.

### 8. What v0.7 is deliberately not

- **Hosting**: v0.8, see Goal.
- **Nested subagents** (`parent_session_id`): rows are flat, like core-tui's bar.
- **Opening a subagent from the bar.** The bar is a glance; `/subagents events <name>` is the drill-in, and it already exists.

- **core-tui's tasks bar** (`tui/tasks_bar.go`) is the reference for §2. Its comments explain each choice, including the ones this plan copies.

## Open questions

1. **Roster poll cadence.** Proposed: ride the status chain (3 s live / 10 s idle), not core-tui's 1 Hz, because a room has many panels. The cost is up to 3 s of staleness on a subagent starting or finishing. Decide in PR 0 with the request count in front of us.
   **Decided as proposed** (#143): one extra read per tick per panel, and nothing while the tab is hidden. An active subagent puts the chain on its live rate, and the read re-arms the chain when it lands, so a start or finish changes the rate on that tick, not the next.
2. **The bar in a small spatial panel.** Proposed: one row plus the count. Decide in PR 1 with it on screen.
   **Decided as proposed** (#145), after screenshots of solo, spatial in front and spatial parked: a parked panel shows one row (`N subagents · /subagents` when there's more than one), and the count stays in its status line.

## At execution

- **Protocol high-water mark: 1.20.0**, as of `core-agent@99a1f46`, the version this release consumes. No drift this time.
- **Real-daemon run** (2026-10-10): the v0.7 browser against `core-agent@99a1f46` (echo provider, through `dev/tools/e2e-real-backend` plus a loopback side server per the rig notes). `e2e-real-backend` passed in full. The browser ran a turn, read `GET /agents` with a 200 on every tick (`{"agents":[]}`, the mock's default shape), drew no bar and no hold count with nothing running, offered the daemon's own command table in the autocomplete, including its advertised `/subagent`, and logged no console errors. A real subagent wasn't spawned: the echo provider can't, and the bar's rows are covered against the mock by smoke 028.
- **One deviation from §5's wording**: the autocomplete opens upward over the transcript, not under the prompt, because the panel's edge clips anything below it.
